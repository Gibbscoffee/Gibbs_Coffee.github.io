03 / Green rituals

Three small habits that keep plants well.

Simple growing advice you can keep. Fixed schedules are only starting points; your plant and your room will tell you the rest.

01

Light before labels

Notice whether your space receives direct sun, bright indirect light or shade. Match the plant to the room rather than forcing the room to suit the plant.

02

Water with attention

Check the soil before watering. Pot size, drainage, heat and season all matter; fixed schedules are only starting points.

03

Let roots breathe

Choose a pot with drainage and a free-draining mix. Empty standing water and watch for changes in leaves as useful signals.

Ask us directly ↗
Where can I find Mandevu at Kwa Nyuki?

Please message us for the exact location and opening arrangements before you travel, so we can make sure someone is there to meet you.

Are the listed prices fixed?

Listed prices are a guide and may change with plant size, pot choice and season. We will confirm the final price when you enquire.

Which plant is best for a beginner?

Snake plants are a gentle start: they prefer well-draining soil and drying out between waterings. Spider plants suit brighter rooms and slightly more regular watering.

Can plants be delivered or only collected?

Ask us. Arrangements depend on the plant, its size and where it is going, and we would rather confirm than promise.

Can I order coffee here too?

Yes, Charlie's Coffee is part of Rustic Garden. See the coffee section below, or visit the coffee page, and use the coffee enquiry address.

The coffee collection

Charlie's Coffee.
Time, held in a cup.

Honey-processed Arabica from the Mount Kenya foothills, associated with Kavutiri, Embu County. In honey processing, some fruit mucilage remains on the bean as it dries, bringing a rounded body and the potential for a naturally sweet, fruit-led cup.

Choose a lighter roast for brighter citrus and floral impressions, or a darker roast for deeper, chocolate-like notes. Your brew method and the beans themselves will shape the final cup.

Community & digital inclusion

From the coffee farm to the global internet.

Rustic Garden's proposed Universal Acceptance Day 2027 concept in Kavutiri, Embu County, explores a practical question: can digital systems make it easier for rural producers to reach the people and markets they choose?

The proposed programme brings farmers, cooperatives, businesses, educators and technology practitioners together to test selected websites, forms and email workflows, document compatibility gaps, and identify practical next steps.

Universal Acceptance helps digital systems correctly accept and process valid domain names and email addresses, including internationalized forms. It does not by itself provide connectivity, market demand or higher prices.
