1. Understanding Your Body's Signals

As we grow older, the body sometimes carries lingering irritation — stiff joints, dry or sensitive skin, or breathing that feels a little tighter than it used to. Many traditions describe this restless, dry energy as an imbalance that benefits from slow, steady touch.

You don't need to fight this feeling. By resting gentle, sustained pressure on a few well-known points on your hands, arms, legs, and ears, you send calming signals along your nerves — a simple, time-honoured way to help your body settle.

A gentle reminder: these are traditional comfort techniques, not medical treatment. If you have an ongoing health condition or something feels new or worsening, please check in with your doctor or nurse.
